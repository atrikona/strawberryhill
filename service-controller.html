Fractional Controller

Bookkeeping, accounting, and payroll together: books kept current, closed on schedule, and your team paid right.

Where it fits

Fractional Controller bundles bookkeeping, accounting, and payroll in one engagement, priced below buying the three separately. Already have a bookkeeper? Our Accounting service covers the close and statements on its own.

What it is

Bookkeeping records every dollar moving through your business. Accounting is what turns those records into something you can use: a monthly close on a set schedule, financial statements you can trust, and someone accountable for the accuracy of the numbers.

That second layer is the controller function. It means the books are reviewed, adjustments are made, and a P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow statement land in your inbox every month in plain terms. If a bank or a buyer asks for numbers, you can send them the same day.

Payroll runs alongside it, so the deposits and filings tie out to the same books. Already have a bookkeeper you like? We offer Accounting on its own, reviewing their work and producing the close and statements. Want all three? Bundling costs less than buying the pieces separately.

Who it’s for
  • Owners who want the books, statements, and payroll handled by one team
  • Businesses with a bookkeeper but no one reviewing or closing the books
  • Owners who can’t get a clear read on whether they made money last month
  • Businesses preparing for a loan, an investor, or a sale
  • Anyone who finds year-end difficult due to the reconstruction work involved
What’s included
  • Everything in Bookkeeping, or review of your existing bookkeeper’s work
  • Payroll processing, tax deposits, and filings
  • Monthly close on a set schedule
  • Profit and loss, balance sheet, and cash flow statements
  • Adjusting entries and account reviews
  • Clean, business-specific chart of accounts
  • Year-end packages ready for filing
  • Internal controls and process improvement
