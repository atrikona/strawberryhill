Advisory Service

Growth & Transition Readiness

Getting your financials in the shape they need to be in before the next step, whatever that step turns out to be.

What it is

Every business eventually faces a moment where someone outside the company looks closely at its numbers. A bank underwriting a loan. An investor considering a stake. A buyer doing diligence. A partner buying in. A family member taking over.

Readiness is the work of being prepared for that moment before it arrives. It means clean, consistent financials with a defensible history, clear separation between business and personal expenses, documented processes, and a story your numbers actually support.

The reason to do this early is leverage. Scrambling to clean things up mid-negotiation signals disorganization, slows the process, and almost always costs you money at the table. Businesses that were already ready simply get better terms. And most of this work makes the business easier to run in the meantime regardless of whether the transition ever happens.

Who it's for

  • Owners thinking about raising capital or taking on debt
  • Businesses planning a significant scale-up
  • Owners considering a sale in the next few years
  • Families planning a generational handoff
  • Businesses bringing on a partner or buying out an existing one
  • Owners who simply want the option available when the time comes

What's included

  • Financial statement cleanup and normalization
  • Separation of personal and business expenses
  • Historical financials rebuilt to a consistent standard
  • Quality of earnings preparation support
  • Documentation of key processes and contracts
  • Identification of issues a buyer or lender would flag
  • Forecast and growth plan to support the conversation

Where it fits

Readiness work is typically a defined project, often running alongside a Full Advisory Partner membership so the improvements are maintained rather than done once and lost.
