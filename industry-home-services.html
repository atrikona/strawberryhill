HVAC, Plumbing & Home Services Accounting

The most actively consolidated trade in the country right now, and the one where clean financials are worth the most.

HVAC & refrigeration · Plumbing · Electrical · Roofing · Landscaping · Pest control

What's happening

Where this industry stands right now.

01

Private equity is buying at scale

Roughly 800 HVAC, plumbing, and electrical companies have been acquired by private equity since 2022. PE buyers now account for about half of all HVAC-services transactions, up from roughly a third the year before.

02

The market is built for roll-ups

Across roughly 120,000 US HVAC contracting businesses, the largest single player holds under 2% market share. That fragmentation is exactly what consolidators look for, and it is why the letters keep arriving.

03

Clean books move the multiple

Two companies with the same $2M EBITDA can clear at 4.5x or 7.5x depending on how clean the working-capital peg is, how well the maintenance-agreement base is documented, and how clean the accounting file is. On $2M, that spread is roughly $6 million.

What it means for you

Where we focus in this industry.

The financial work that actually moves the needle here, rather than a generic checklist.

  • ✓Recurring maintenance-agreement revenue has to be documented as recurring, not buried in general service income.
  • ✓Work in progress and deferred revenue need consistent treatment month to month, not just at year end.
  • ✓Owner compensation and personal expenses have to be cleanly separated well before anyone looks closely.
  • ✓Job-level costing tells you which work is actually profitable, which most operators are guessing at.
