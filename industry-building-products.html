What’s happening

Where this industry stands right now.

Roofing is consolidating at an extraordinary pace

PE-backed roofing platforms grew from 17 to 56 during 2024, and private equity now owns 7 of the 10 largest US roofing contractors. By mid-2025, a US roofing platform was being acquired roughly every 48 hours.

Distribution is consolidating too

Home Depot's $18.25B acquisition of SRS and QXO's $11B deal for Beacon reshaped the supply chain above you. Consolidation upstream changes your purchasing leverage and your margins.

Buyers are vetting more rigorously

Multiples sit at the high end of historical ranges, and buyers are underwriting more carefully as a result: normalized EBITDA, backlog quality, recurring service revenue, crew continuity, and whether the business runs without the founder holding it together.

What it means for you

Where we focus in this industry.

The financial work that actually moves the needle here, rather than a generic checklist.

  • Backlog and work in progress need to be schedulable and defensible, not estimated from memory.
  • Percentage-of-completion accounting has to be applied consistently, or earnings look volatile when they aren't.
  • Material cost volatility needs to show up in your forecast before it shows up in your margins.
  • Storm and seasonal revenue should be normalized so a buyer or lender sees the real earnings base.

Accurate inventory and cost-of-goods tracking, payroll for production and installation crews, and margin reporting by product line and job. Those are the numbers that drive pricing and growth decisions.
