What we do

Accounting and finance, handled.

Accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, and tax keep the business accurate and current. Finance is what turns those numbers into a plan: someone looking at where the business is headed, not just where it's been.

What it is

Transaction-level work: purchases, deposits and transfers coded correctly and matched against what your bank actually shows.

What you get

No more screens of uncategorized transactions. Books close on schedule, and tax season stops being a reconstruction project.

Why it matters

This is the foundation everything else is built on. Current books cost far less to maintain than late books cost to repair.

Included

  • ✓Bank and credit card reconciliation
  • ✓Transaction categorization and coding
  • ✓Accounts receivable and payable tracking
  • ✓QuickBooks Online setup and support
  • ✓Catch-up work if you're behind
