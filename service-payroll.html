Core Service

Payroll

Your team paid correctly and on time, with the filings that go along with it handled.

What it is

Payroll looks simple until it isn't. Every paycheck carries withholding obligations, deposit deadlines, and filings attached to it, and the penalties for getting those wrong compound quietly.

We run payroll on your schedule, make the tax deposits, file the required forms, and handle contractor payments and 1099s. You find out about a problem from us, not from a notice six months later.

Who it's for

  • Businesses with W-2 employees on any schedule
  • Owners paying themselves a salary through an S-corp
  • Businesses that use 1099 contractors regularly
  • Owners currently running payroll manually or through a tool they don't fully trust
  • Anyone who has received a payroll tax notice

What's included

  • Payroll processing on your schedule
  • Federal, state, and local payroll tax deposits
  • Quarterly and annual payroll tax filings
  • W-2 and 1099 preparation and distribution
  • New hire reporting
  • Contractor payment tracking

Where it fits

Payroll is part of the Books & Payroll add-on and is included in the Full Advisory Partner membership.
