Why this matters

Most CPAs only show up once a year, to file the return.

By the time they see your numbers, the year is already over. Filing on time is necessary, but it only tells you what already happened. Companies that grow steadily are typically the ones that also understand what's coming next.

See a problem before it arrives

A cash flow forecast identifies a tight month 90 days in advance, rather than the week payroll is due.

Know what you can actually afford

A proper budget turns "can we afford to hire" from a guess into a decision grounded in your numbers.

Be ready for what's next

Raising capital, scaling the business, or eventually selling all require financials that hold up under outside scrutiny. That readiness is built over time, not assembled at the last moment.
