Why Strawberry Hill

Strawberry Hill is one of Kansas City’s oldest neighborhoods, founded by immigrant families who arrived with little and started businesses meant to last.

That same spirit still defines the businesses we work with today: owners who put their name behind their business, work hard to grow it, and want to create something lasting.

The name is the standard we hold ourselves to.
