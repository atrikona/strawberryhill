Understanding the value

Because “fine” can turn into a cash crunch quickly, and it's usually invisible until it has already happened. Growth is often the culprit: it consumes cash faster than it produces it. A forecast shows you that in advance, while you still have options.

It's the foundation everything else sits on. Pricing, hiring, what you pay yourself, whether a lender approves you, what a buyer would pay: all of it comes from your books. If they're inaccurate, every one of those decisions is a guess.
