What’s happening

Where this industry stands right now.

A generational transfer is underway

McKinsey estimates roughly six million US small and midsize businesses will face ownership transitions by 2035, representing up to $5 trillion in enterprise value. A large share are family-held manufacturers, fabricators, and distributors.

The Midwest is a target market

Private equity views the region as attractive hunting ground: valuations are more reasonable than coastal markets, and family-owned businesses present operational upside. Buyers are specifically interested in precision machining, metal fabrication, plastics, and companies serving automotive, ag equipment, and heavy machinery.

Supply is about to outrun demand

With this many businesses coming to market at once, buyers get to be selective. The premium goes to companies that are prepared, professionally presented, and clearly differentiated. The unprepared compete on price.

What it means for you

Where we focus in this industry.

The financial work that actually moves the needle here, rather than a generic checklist.

  • Inventory valuation and costing methods need to hold up under diligence, not just satisfy the return.
  • Customer concentration is the single most common valuation discount, and it can be managed if you see it early.
  • Equipment depreciation and capex planning belong in a forecast, not a year-end conversation.
  • Owner dependence has to be reduced deliberately, over years, before a buyer prices it in.

Common questions

Common questions, answered.

What accounting do manufacturers and distributors need?

Reliable cost accounting and inventory records, payroll for production teams, and reporting that shows margin by product and customer. That is the foundation for pricing and investment decisions.

Can you help with succession planning for a family-owned manufacturer?

Yes. We help get financials ready for a transition and can provide valuations so owners and successors work from the same numbers.

Do you offer CFO-level support for manufacturers?

Yes. Our fractional CFO and FP&A work gives manufacturers forecasting, budgeting, and scenario planning without hiring a full-time CFO.

Where do you work with manufacturing clients?

We work with manufacturers, distributors, and other founder-led businesses across the Midwest.
