Fractional CFO

Senior financial leadership on a part-time basis, for businesses that need the judgment but not the full-time salary.

What it is

A full-time CFO is out of reach for most founder-led businesses, and often more capacity than they'd use. A fractional arrangement gives you the same seniority of judgment on a defined schedule.

In practice this means someone in the room for the decisions that matter: pricing, financing, major hires, expansion, partner conversations, lender negotiations. It's the FP&A work plus the standing relationship and the willingness to tell you when a plan doesn't hold up.

Who it's for

  • Businesses past the point where the owner can carry finance alone
  • Companies preparing for a financing round or major expansion
  • Owners who want a second set of eyes on big decisions
  • Businesses with a bookkeeper but no strategic finance function
  • Companies navigating a complex or uncertain period

What's included

  • Everything in Financial Planning & Analysis
  • Regular leadership meetings on a set cadence
  • Lender and investor reporting packages
  • Decision support for major initiatives
  • Financial systems and process improvement
  • Direct availability between scheduled sessions

Where it fits

Fractional CFO service is the most involved version of the Full Advisory Partner membership, scoped to your business and its cadence.
